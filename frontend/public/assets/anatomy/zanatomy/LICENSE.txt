Changes made by MEDVERSE: the meshes were exported from the Z-Anatomy FBX files,
selected (head structures absent from the BodyParts3D build), registered to the
BodyParts3D skull by a similarity transform, renamed to FMA identifiers, re-bundled
and meshopt-compressed. The female-* files were additionally fitted to the HRA
Visible Human Female head by a bounded affine transform: they are an approximation
derived from a male model, NOT female anatomy.
